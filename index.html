What's in Endzone Kings

Every mode is free and works on computer and phone.

Ultimate Team

Open packs, train players, build a lineup and trade cards with real players. Take on Solo Battles, monthly theme challenges and program rewards.

Online H2H

Play a friend with a game code or anyone from the lobby. Your Online H2H record and rank only count games against real people.

Online Franchise

Start a public or private league with up to 16 players, then draft, trade and play your way to the Kings Bowl. Unlocks at level 5.

Season and Franchise

Pick a team and chase a title against the CPU over a full season, or run a franchise for years with drafts, contracts and progression.

Ranked and rewards

Climb from Bronze to Kings, earn coins and XP, claim daily rewards, finish challenges and unlock achievements and celebrations.

Community

Live leaderboards, player profiles, followers, messages, clips of your best plays, and watching friends' games live.

About Habir Studios

Habir Studios is a small, independent game studio. Our team handles everything ourselves: design, art, code, testing and listening to players.

We believe great games should be easy to get to. That means free to play, nothing to download, no ads and nothing to buy. Just open your browser on a computer or a phone and play, and bring your friends along.

We build in the open and update constantly. Players tell us what to fix and what to add, and a lot of what ends up in our games started as a message from someone playing them. Every change goes into the patch notes so you always know what's new.

We care about fair play and a friendly community. That means anti-cheat that tells the difference between skill, lag and actual cheating, moderation that takes reports seriously, and accounts and data handled with care. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service anytime.

Got an idea, found a bug, or just want to say what's up? Contact us. We read everything.
